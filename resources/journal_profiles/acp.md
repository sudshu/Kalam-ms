# Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics (ACP)

## Overview
- **Full name**: Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics
- **Publisher**: Copernicus Publications (EGU)
- **Scope**: Atmospheric chemistry, physics, and dynamics. Earth's atmosphere including interactions with biosphere, hydrosphere, cryosphere, and lithosphere.
- **Audience**: Atmospheric scientists, climate researchers
- **Impact Factor**: ~6 (2024)
- **Open Access**: Yes (Gold OA, CC-BY)
- **Special feature**: Two-stage open peer review (discussion paper → final paper)

## Article Types
| Type | Word Limit | Figures | Abstract |
|------|-----------|---------|----------|
| Research Article | No strict limit (~8,000-12,000 words typical) | No strict limit | 250 words |
| Review | No strict limit | No strict limit | 250 words |
| Technical Note | ~4,000 words | 4-6 display items | 200 words |

## Formatting
- **Sections**: Abstract, Introduction, Data/Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions, Appendix (optional), Data Availability, Author Contributions, Competing Interests, Acknowledgments, References.
- **Data Availability**: REQUIRED. Must provide DOIs for datasets.
- **Author Contributions**: REQUIRED.
- **References**: Author-year style (Copernicus house style).
- **Citation style**: Author-year, e.g., "(Author et al., 2024)" or "Author et al. (2024)"
- **Appendix**: For supplementary material (not separate SI — it's part of the paper).
- **Line numbering**: Required for review stage.

## LaTeX
- **Document class**: `copernicus` class (`copernicus.cls`)
- **Bibliography style**: `copernicus.bst`
- **Template**: Copernicus provides official LaTeX template.

## Writing Style
- Detailed and thorough — ACP allows longer papers with extensive methods
- Technical depth valued over brevity
- Separate Results and Discussion sections (not combined)
- Conclusions section expected and can be detailed
- Open peer review means reviewers are named — reviews tend to be constructive
- Discussion papers are publicly visible during review

## What Reviewers Look For
- Sound atmospheric science with proper uncertainty quantification
- Thorough methodology description
- Appropriate use of observational/model data
- Clear connection to atmospheric processes
- Proper comparison with existing literature

## Tips for Acceptance
- ACP allows more space for methods — use it for reproducibility
- The two-stage review is unique: the discussion paper is public, so make the initial submission strong
- Reviewer comments and author responses are public — be thorough in responses
- Good for detailed methodological contributions
- Open access + open review → high transparency → builds trust → more citations
- Appendices are part of the main paper (not separate SI)
