# Geophysical Research Letters (GRL)

## Overview
- **Full name**: Geophysical Research Letters
- **Publisher**: American Geophysical Union (Wiley)
- **Scope**: Short, high-impact communications in all areas of Earth and space sciences. Emphasizes broad significance and rapid publication.
- **Audience**: Broad Earth and space science community
- **Impact Factor**: ~5 (2024)

## Article Types
| Type | Word Limit | Figures | Abstract |
|------|-----------|---------|----------|
| Research Letter | 4,000 words (main text only) | 4 display items + unlimited SI | 150 words |
| Commentary | 2,000 words | 2 display items | 100 words |

## Formatting
- **Sections**: Abstract, Plain Language Summary, Key Points (3), Introduction, Methods/Data, Results, Discussion/Conclusions, Acknowledgments, Data Availability, References.
- **Plain Language Summary**: REQUIRED. ~200 words. No jargon.
- **Key Points**: REQUIRED. Exactly 3 bullet points, max 140 characters each.
- **References**: Author-year (AGU style). Max ~30-40 for letters.
- **Citation style**: Author-year, e.g., "(Author et al., 2024)"
- **Data Availability**: REQUIRED with DOIs.
- **Supporting Information**: Unlimited — detailed methods, extra figures, tables.

## LaTeX
- **Document class**: `agujournal2019` with option `\journalname{Geophysical Research Letters}`
- **Bibliography style**: `agufull08.bst`
- **Template**: AGU official LaTeX template.

## Writing Style
- Extremely concise — every sentence must count
- 4,000 words is tight: ~800 intro, ~800 methods, ~1200 results, ~800 discussion, ~400 conclusions
- Move detailed methods to Supporting Information
- Figures should be information-dense but clear
- Fast-paced narrative — get to the point quickly
- Opening sentence should immediately signal significance

## What Reviewers Look For
- Significance and timeliness
- Concise, focused presentation
- Clear advance over prior work
- Appropriate scope for a letter (not trying to do too much)
- Strong Key Points and PLS

## Tips for Acceptance
- GRL is about speed and significance — be concise
- Key Points are widely read — make them impactful
- Supporting Information can carry detailed methods, extra analyses
- 4,000 words forces you to be focused — this is a feature, not a bug
- Strong opening paragraph is critical
- GRL values novelty and timeliness over completeness
- One major finding, clearly demonstrated, is enough
