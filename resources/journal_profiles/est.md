# Environmental Science & Technology (ES&T)

## Overview
- **Full name**: Environmental Science & Technology
- **Publisher**: American Chemical Society (ACS)
- **Scope**: Environmental science, engineering, and policy. Covers pollution, remediation, climate, water, air quality, and environmental health.
- **Audience**: Environmental scientists, engineers, and policymakers
- **Impact Factor**: ~11 (2024)

## Article Types
| Type | Word Limit | Figures | Abstract |
|------|-----------|---------|----------|
| Article | 7,000 words (incl. refs) | 6 display items | 200 words |

## Formatting
- **Sections**: Abstract, Introduction, Methods/Experimental Section, Results and Discussion (typically combined), Implications/Environmental Implications, Acknowledgments, References.
- **Environmental Implications**: REQUIRED for Articles. A separate paragraph (100-150 words) explaining the environmental significance. Appears after the abstract.
- **TOC/Abstract Art**: REQUIRED. A graphical abstract (table of contents image) that visually summarizes the work.
- **References**: ACS style — numbered in order of appearance, superscript.
- **Citation style**: Numbered, superscript
- **SI**: Supporting Information for additional data/figures

## LaTeX
- **Document class**: `achemso` (ACS document class)
- **Bibliography style**: `achemso.bst`
- **Template**: ACS provides official LaTeX template via `achemso` package.

## Writing Style
- Environmental relevance must be front and center
- Results and Discussion typically combined
- Environmental Implications statement is unique to ES&T — highlight real-world relevance
- Quantitative and specific
- Policy connections valued but not required
- Graphical abstract should be eye-catching and self-explanatory

## What Reviewers Look For
- Clear environmental relevance
- Rigorous analytical/modeling methodology
- Novel contribution to environmental understanding
- Practical implications
- Strong graphical abstract

## Tips for Acceptance
- The Environmental Implications statement is critical — make it compelling
- Graphical abstract gets shared on social media → design it well
- ES&T values practical environmental applications
- Connect to regulatory frameworks or environmental standards when possible
- Combined Results and Discussion works well for concise storytelling
- Word limit includes references — plan accordingly
