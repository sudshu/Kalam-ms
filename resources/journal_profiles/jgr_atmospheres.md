# JGR-Atmospheres (Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres)

## Overview
- **Full name**: Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres
- **Publisher**: American Geophysical Union (Wiley)
- **Scope**: Comprehensive research in atmospheric sciences: composition, chemistry, dynamics, radiation, climate, weather, aerosols, clouds, and boundary layer processes.
- **Audience**: Atmospheric science specialists
- **Impact Factor**: ~4 (2024)

## Article Types
| Type | Word Limit | Figures | Abstract |
|------|-----------|---------|----------|
| Research Article | No strict limit (~8,000-12,000 words typical) | No strict limit (8-12 typical) | 250 words |
| Technical Report: Methods | No strict limit | No strict limit | 250 words |
| Technical Report: Data | No strict limit | No strict limit | 250 words |
| Review | No strict limit | No strict limit | 250 words |
| Commentary | ~3,000 words | 3 display items | 150 words |

## Formatting
- **Sections**: Abstract, Plain Language Summary, Key Points (3), Introduction, Data/Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions, Acknowledgments, Data Availability, References.
- **Plain Language Summary**: REQUIRED. ~200 words.
- **Key Points**: REQUIRED. 3 bullet points, max 140 characters each.
- **References**: Author-year (AGU style). No strict limit.
- **Citation style**: Author-year, e.g., "(Author et al., 2024)"
- **Data Availability**: REQUIRED with DOIs.
- **Supporting Information**: For supplementary analyses.

## LaTeX
- **Document class**: `agujournal2019` with option `\journalname{JGR: Atmospheres}`
- **Bibliography style**: `agufull08.bst`
- **Template**: AGU official LaTeX template.

## Writing Style
- Detailed and thorough — JGR allows comprehensive treatment
- Full methodology description in main text
- Separate Results and Discussion sections
- Conclusions can be detailed (3-5 paragraphs)
- Technical depth valued — this is a specialist journal
- Equations and mathematical framework welcome

## What Reviewers Look For
- Technical rigor and methodological soundness
- Comprehensive treatment of the topic
- Proper uncertainty characterization
- Thorough comparison with prior work
- Clear Key Points and PLS

## Tips for Acceptance
- JGR-Atmos is the workhouse journal for atmospheric science — solid, thorough papers
- No word limit means you can be comprehensive with methods
- Good for detailed methodological contributions
- Technical Reports: Methods/Data are specifically for method papers and datasets
- Key Points and PLS improve discoverability
- Multiple revision rounds are normal — be patient and thorough in responses
- Strong for papers that are technically important but maybe not "flashy" enough for GRL
