# Remote Sensing of Environment (RSE)

## Overview
- **Full name**: Remote Sensing of Environment
- **Publisher**: Elsevier
- **Scope**: Theory, science, applications, and technology of remote sensing of Earth's environment. Emphasizes integration of remote sensing with environmental science.
- **Audience**: Remote sensing scientists, Earth observation community
- **Impact Factor**: ~13 (2024)

## Formatting
- **Sections**: Abstract, Keywords (4-6), Introduction, Study Area/Data, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions, Acknowledgments, References.
- **Highlights**: REQUIRED. 3-5 bullet points (max 85 characters each) summarizing key findings.
- **Keywords**: REQUIRED (4-6 keywords).
- **Graphical Abstract**: Optional but recommended.
- **References**: Author-year style (Elsevier house style).
- **Citation style**: Author-year, e.g., "(Author et al., 2024)"
- **Appendix**: For supplementary material.
- **CRediT Author Statement**: REQUIRED.

## LaTeX
- **Document class**: `elsarticle` (Elsevier article class)
- **Bibliography style**: `elsarticle-harv.bst` (Harvard/author-year)
- **Template**: Elsevier provides official LaTeX template.

## Writing Style
- Technical and detailed — RSE readers expect methodological depth
- Study Area section is common (describe the geographic context)
- Validation/accuracy assessment is critical for remote sensing papers
- Comparison with other retrieval methods/products expected
- Error analysis and uncertainty characterization valued

## What Reviewers Look For
- Novel remote sensing method or application
- Rigorous validation against independent data
- Clear improvement over existing methods/products
- Practical applicability for environmental monitoring
- Comprehensive error/uncertainty analysis
- Reproducibility (data, code, parameters)

## Tips for Acceptance
- Highlights are indexed and searchable — write them as standalone findings
- Validation section is often the most scrutinized — be thorough
- Include comparison with existing satellite products/methods
- RSE values operational relevance — how can this be used in practice?
- Keywords are used by Elsevier's recommendation engine — choose carefully
- Maps and spatial figures should be publication-quality with proper projections
