# AGU Advances

## Overview
- **Full name**: AGU Advances
- **Publisher**: American Geophysical Union (Wiley)
- **Scope**: High-impact, cross-disciplinary research in Earth and space sciences. AGU's flagship open-access journal for transformative research.
- **Audience**: Broad Earth and space science community
- **Impact Factor**: ~12 (2024)
- **Open Access**: Yes (Gold OA, CC-BY)

## Article Types
| Type | Word Limit | Figures | Abstract |
|------|-----------|---------|----------|
| Research Article | No strict limit (~6,000 words typical) | No strict limit (6-8 typical) | 250 words |
| Review | No strict limit | No strict limit | 250 words |
| Commentary | ~3,000 words | 3 display items | 150 words |
| Perspective | ~4,000 words | 4 display items | 200 words |

## Formatting
- **Sections**: Abstract, Plain Language Summary (PLS), Key Points (3 bullet points), Introduction, Methods/Data, Results, Discussion, Conclusions, Acknowledgments, Data Availability, References.
- **Plain Language Summary**: REQUIRED. ~200 words. Accessible to non-specialists. No jargon, no citations, no acronyms.
- **Key Points**: REQUIRED. Exactly 3 bullet points, each max 140 characters. Summarize the main findings.
- **References**: Author-year style (AGU style). No strict limit but typically 40-80.
- **Citation style**: Author-year, e.g., "(Author et al., 2024)" or "Author et al. (2024)"
- **Data Availability**: REQUIRED. Must cite data/code repositories with DOIs.

## LaTeX
- **Document class**: `agujournal2019` with option `\journalname{AGU Advances}`
- **Bibliography style**: `agufull08.bst` (AGU bibliography style)
- **Template**: AGU provides official LaTeX template.

## Writing Style
- Accessible but technically rigorous
- Emphasis on cross-disciplinary impact within Earth/space sciences
- Plain Language Summary forces accessibility
- Key Points are widely read — make them count
- Data availability and open science strongly valued

## What Reviewers Look For
- Cross-disciplinary significance within Earth/space sciences
- Novel approach or finding that advances the field broadly
- Strong data availability and reproducibility
- Clear Plain Language Summary and Key Points
- Well-designed figures that tell the story

## Tips for Acceptance
- Key Points are like a mini-abstract — optimize them for impact
- PLS is indexed by search engines — include keywords
- Open access means broader reach — optimize for discoverability
- AGU Advances values papers that bridge subfields (e.g., connecting remote sensing to carbon cycle to climate policy)
- Supporting Information can be extensive
- Strong figures are especially important — AGU readers are visually oriented
