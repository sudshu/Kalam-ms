# Ideation — demo-carbon-debt

Stage 1 output. Approved before the skeleton was written.

## The question

Do repeated hot-dry extremes leave tropical forests with less ecosystem carbon after 20
years than the same background climate trajectory without the extreme anomalies?

## The one-sentence answer

Yes in this model — and the deficit is invisible to the flux-recovery diagnostic
normally used to declare the forest recovered.

## Why it is worth a paper

The field's standard recovery metric measures the wrong quantity. Not wrongly measured:
*a different quantity*. Showing that the two diverge, and by how much, is a result that
changes how an existing metric should be reported.

## The narrative arc

1. Tropical forests dominate land-sink variability; compound hot-dry extremes are
   becoming more frequent.
2. Recovery is judged from flux return time, because that is what we can observe.
3. Flux return says nothing about whether the lost carbon was replaced — that needs a
   counterfactual, which cannot be observed.
4. Build the counterfactual in a model, with the extremes spliced out.
5. Fluxes recover in months; stocks do not recover in 20 years.
6. The gap is foregone productivity, which by construction leaves no anomalous efflux
   to detect.
7. Report flux recovery and stock comparison separately.

## Claims and the evidence each rests on

| Claim | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Fluxes recover in 6–18 months | Composite NBP anomaly, 80% of events | strong within the model |
| Stocks remain lower at 20 yr | 73% of 800 simulations positive; pooled median 1.9 | sign robust, magnitude not |
| Not an artefact of splicing | Neutral-splice placebo at 0.05 | strong |
| Mostly foregone productivity | Factorial decomposition, 68% NPP | model-internal only |

## Known weaknesses to state, not hide

- One model structure, four sites.
- Pooled 95% interval spans zero — claim the sign, not the size.
- No observational test of 20-year stock differences is possible for this design.
- Site ordering is a property of a percentile-based extreme definition.

## Decisions taken at this stage

- Target *Nature Communications*; the result is methodological-interpretive rather than
  a new global number, which fits the journal better than *Nature*.
- Captions descriptive, not assertion-led: the claim is already contested enough.
- Robustness tests go to SI; only the placebo stays in the main text, because it is the
  test that decides whether the result exists at all.
